# RUN-001 Source / Canonical Tensions

## CONFLICT-001 — Scope of “Hallucination”

- **S04 framing (pp. 38–41):** attributes a broad “hallucinated” / “dreaming machine” framing to Karpathy and presents generation as possibly true, false, creative, or truth-neutral. The slides argue that generation itself is the feature, while users object mainly to unsupported output from an assistant.
- **Read-only permanent canonical framing:** `Hallucination.md` defines hallucination more narrowly as plausible-looking output that lacks grounding or conflicts with facts, while separately acknowledging that creative generation may not have a truth value.
- **Difference:** the materials largely agree on the mechanism but use the term at different scopes. S04 sometimes uses “hallucination” for generated continuation broadly; the permanent concept reserves it primarily for the reliability risk.
- **Staging treatment:** preserve the S04 framing as attributed course perspective, retain the narrower reliability-risk definition as the likely neutral canonical scope, and do not publish a merged definition until the user confirms the treatment.
- **Confirmation gate:** mandatory before publication of the Hallucination concept or any teaching path that uses this conflict.

No other direct contradiction was found in RUN-001. Differences in detail—such as S04's simplified Transformer explanation—are recorded as incompleteness rather than conflict.
