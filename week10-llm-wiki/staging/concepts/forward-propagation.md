# Forward Propagation（前向傳播）

## One-sentence Definition / 一句話定義

Forward Propagation（前向傳播）passes an input through successive network operations to produce a prediction and loss. Source: S05 pp. 5, 14–15.

## Why It Matters / 為什麼重要

It is the prediction half of the training loop and supplies the values used to measure error before gradient computation. Source: S05 pp. 5, 14–15.
