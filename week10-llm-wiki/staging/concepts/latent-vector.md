# Latent Vector（潛在向量）

## One-sentence Definition / 一句話定義

A Latent Vector（潛在向量） is an internal numeric representation whose values can encode variation not directly specified by the visible input.

Source: S03, pp. 9–12, 24–32.

## Why It Matters / 為什麼重要

Randomly sampling latent values gives a generator a controllable source of output diversity.

Source: S03, pp. 9–12, 24–32.
