# Hallucination（幻覺）

## One-sentence Definition / 一句話定義

Hallucination（幻覺） is framed in S04 as generated continuation that may be unsupported, false, creative, or not meaningfully judged as true or false.

Source: S04, pp. 38–41.

## Source Perspectives / 來源觀點

S03 records the claim that generative AI hallucinates and discriminative AI should therefore be used, but immediately cautions that the two task framings share underlying principles. This is treated as a warning that the label alone does not guarantee reliability, not as a new definition of Hallucination.

Source: S03, p. 98.

S09 defines hallucination more narrowly as fluent output that is ungrounded or incorrect and places it among broader LLM risks and evaluation limitations. This narrower reliability use is preserved beside S04's broader creative-generation framing.

Source: S09, book pp. 29–31.

## Why It Matters / 為什麼重要

It separates fluent generation from factual retrieval or verification.

Source: S04, pp. 38–41.

## Core Idea / 核心概念

S04 attributes a “dreaming machine” framing to Karpathy and uses creative generations to show that generation itself is not equivalent to factual lookup. This attributed framing is not converted into the claim that factual errors are harmless.

Source: S04, pp. 38–41.
