# Large Language Model（大型語言模型）

## One-sentence Definition / 一句話定義

A Large Language Model（大型語言模型） is presented in S04 as a scaled language model that generates text through repeated next-unit prediction.

Source: S04, pp. 39–47.

## Why It Matters / 為什麼重要

The scale framing explains how one predictive objective can support broad text continuation and downstream use.

Source: S04, pp. 39–47.

## Source Perspectives / 來源觀點

S09 expands the course's scale framing into an explicit lifecycle: tokenization and Transformer pretraining, post-training, and inference. Time-sensitive product rankings and model-size claims are excluded.

Source: S09, book pp. 5–24, 177–223.
