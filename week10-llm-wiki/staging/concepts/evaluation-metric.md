# Evaluation Metric（評估指標）

## One-sentence Definition / 一句話定義

An Evaluation Metric（評估指標）summarizes model performance for monitoring or assessment and need not be the optimized loss. Source: S05 pp. 36–39; S06 pp. 3–26, 29–89.

## Why It Matters / 為什麼重要

Metrics translate predictions into task-relevant evidence such as RMSE, accuracy, precision, or recall. Source: S05 pp. 36–39.
