# GAN Variants（GAN 變體）

## One-sentence Definition / 一句話定義

GAN Variants（GAN 變體） adapt the basic adversarial design to improve resolution, control style, or translate between image domains.

Source: S03, pp. 36–58.

## Why It Matters / 為什麼重要

The variants show how one generative mechanism can be specialized for distinct data and control requirements.

Source: S03, pp. 36–58.

## Prerequisites / 先備概念

- [[generative-adversarial-network]]
- Paired and unpaired data（成對與非成對資料）

Source basis: S03, pp. 36–58.

## Core Idea / 核心概念

S03 groups Progressive GAN, StyleGAN, Pix2Pix, and CycleGAN. Their details differ—progressive resolution, style-space control, paired translation, or cycle-consistent unpaired translation—so the variants are not presented as interchangeable.

Source: S03, pp. 36–58.
