# Activation Function（激活函數）

## One-sentence Definition / 一句話定義

An Activation Function（激活函數）transforms a neuron's weighted sum, often introducing the nonlinearity needed for multilayer learning. Source: S02 pp. 33–39; S05 pp. 16–20.

## Why It Matters / 為什麼重要

The course maps task/layer roles to functions such as ReLU for hidden layers, sigmoid for binary outputs, and Softmax for multiclass outputs. Source: S05 pp. 16–20; S06 pp. 29–44, 59–71.
