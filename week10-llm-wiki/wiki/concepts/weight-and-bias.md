# Weight and Bias（權重與偏差）

## One-sentence Definition / 一句話定義

Weights（權重）scale incoming signals and a Bias（偏差）shifts their sum before activation. Source: S02 pp. 33–39; S05 pp. 3–5.

## Why It Matters / 為什麼重要

They are trainable parameters whose values determine the function represented by a neural unit or layer. Source: S02 pp. 47–50, 59–75.

## Prerequisites / 先備概念

- Numeric inputs and weighted sums.

## Core Idea / 核心概念

Changing weights changes input influence; the bias allows a response to shift away from an origin-only boundary. The sources support the computational role, not a complete geometric derivation. Source: S02 pp. 33–39; S05 pp. 3–5.
