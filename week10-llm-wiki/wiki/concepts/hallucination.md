# Hallucination（幻覺）

## One-sentence Definition / 一句話定義

Hallucination（幻覺）is fluent model output that is ungrounded or incorrect.

Source: S09, book pp. 29–31. Provenance: Reliable Educational Reference.

## Source Perspectives / 來源觀點

S03 records the claim that generative AI hallucinates and discriminative AI should therefore be used, but immediately cautions that the two task framings share underlying principles. This is treated as a warning that the label alone does not guarantee reliability, not as a new definition of Hallucination.

Source: S03, p. 98.

S09 supplies the canonical technical definition and places hallucination among broader LLM risks and evaluation limitations.

Source: S09, book pp. 29–31.

## Course Perspective / 課程觀點

S04 uses a broader generative/creative framing: continuation may be false, creative, or not meaningfully judged as true or false. This remains an explicitly attributed Course Material perspective and is not merged into the narrower canonical definition.

Source: S04, pp. 38–41. Provenance: Course Material.

## Why It Matters / 為什麼重要

It separates fluent generation from factual retrieval or verification.

Source: S09, book pp. 29–31; S04, pp. 38–41.

Source basis: S04, pp. 38–41.

## Core Idea / 核心概念

The canonical technical scope concerns ungrounded or incorrect output. S04's attributed “dreaming machine” framing remains useful for showing that generation is not factual lookup, but creative output without a truth value is not automatically labelled a technical hallucination.

Source: S09, book pp. 29–31; S04, pp. 38–41.
