# Learning Rate（學習率）

## One-sentence Definition / 一句話定義

The Learning Rate（學習率）scales how far an optimizer updates parameters in response to gradient information. Source: S02 pp. 77–104; S05 pp. 40–64.

## Why It Matters / 為什麼重要

It affects whether training progresses stably, slowly, or overshoots useful parameter regions. Source: S02 pp. 82–104; S05 pp. 40–64.
