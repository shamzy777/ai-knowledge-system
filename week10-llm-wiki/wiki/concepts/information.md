# Information（資訊量）

## One-sentence Definition / 一句話定義

In S03's information-theoretic framing, an event's information content is `I(x) = -log P(x)`.

Source: S03, pp. 65–67.

## Why It Matters / 為什麼重要

The formula makes rare events more informative and forms the building block for entropy and cross entropy.

Source: S03, pp. 65–75.

## Prerequisites / 先備概念

- [[distribution]]
- Logarithm（對數）

Source basis: S03, pp. 65–67.

## Core Idea / 核心概念

Because probability is inside a negative logarithm, lower-probability events receive larger information values.

Source: S03, pp. 65–67.
