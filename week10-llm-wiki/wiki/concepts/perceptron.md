# Perceptron（感知器）

## One-sentence Definition / 一句話定義

A Perceptron（感知器） combines weighted inputs and a bias, then applies an activation/decision rule to produce an output. Source: S02 pp. 33–39; S05 pp. 3–6.

## Why It Matters / 為什麼重要

It is the simplest course model for understanding how neural units transform inputs and why multilayer networks are needed for nonlinear boundaries. Source: S05 pp. 6–8.

## Prerequisites / 先備概念

- [[weight-and-bias]], [[activation-function]]

## Core Idea / 核心概念

S05 contrasts a single unit's linearly separable capacity with an MLP's ability to model XOR-like boundaries. S01 independently sketches prediction, loss, and weight update but is handwritten. Source: S05 pp. 3–8; S01 p. 6.
