# LLM Evaluation（大型語言模型評估）

## One-sentence Definition / 一句話定義

LLM Evaluation（大型語言模型評估） assesses whether a model performs well for a defined task, not merely whether it ranks highly on general benchmarks.

Source: S04, pp. 88–90.

## Why It Matters / 為什麼重要

A high aggregate benchmark score may not predict usefulness for the reader's own tasks.

Source: S04, pp. 88–90.

## Source Perspectives / 來源觀點

S09 broadens evaluation to perplexity, benchmark contamination, human judgment, model-based judges, proxy tasks, and compute tradeoffs while cautioning that every method has limitations.

Source: S09, book pp. 25–28.

## Core Idea / 核心概念

S04 recommends designing standard test prompts for material the evaluator understands well enough to judge. This source does not provide a formal metric framework.

Source: S04, pp. 88–90.
