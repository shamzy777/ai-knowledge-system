# Entropy（熵）

## One-sentence Definition / 一句話定義

Entropy（熵） is the expected information content of outcomes under a probability distribution.

Source: S03, pp. 72–75.

## Why It Matters / 為什麼重要

It provides the reference uncertainty term used to interpret cross entropy and KL divergence.

Source: S03, pp. 72–87.

## Prerequisites / 先備概念

- [[distribution]]

Source basis: S03, pp. 72–75.

## Core Idea / 核心概念

S03 expresses entropy as the probability-weighted average of `-log P(x)` over possible outcomes.

Source: S03, pp. 72–75.
