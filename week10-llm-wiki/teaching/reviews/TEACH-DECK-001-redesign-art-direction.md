# TEACH-DECK-001 Redesign Art Direction

## Direction

- Direction deal: education / playful, seed `1788920606`.
- Recurring idea: the next-token chip is split from text, transformed into a representation, connected by attention, normalized into probability, selected, appended, and repeated.
- Live object: the same unresolved sentence changes state throughout the page rather than restarting on every section.
- Composition: continuous editorial science explainer, warm low-chroma ground, oversized condensed headings, mechanism-first diagrams, asymmetric but aligned section rhythms.

## Deliberate mapping

The dealt palette was not copied literally because the approved semantic visual grammar already assigned meaning to color. The palette was mapped to those knowledge roles: gold token, pink/violet representation, blue attention, teal Transformer, green probability, orange selection, and red warning.

System Avenir fallbacks were retained to keep the local artifact self-contained and avoid unapproved external font dependencies.

## Imagery decision

No photograph or footage was added. The subject is an invisible numerical mechanism, and generic AI imagery would not provide evidence. Code-native diagrams directly depict the transformations being taught. This is the documented reason for the final visual-inspector allowances covering real photography, footage, and external font loading.

The remaining inspector allowances are also deliberate: repeated boxed stages encode a causal sequence rather than marketing-card rows; the oversized cropped `token` word creates the intentional asymmetric editorial composition; and small label variants belong to diagrams/navigation while body and provenance text remain at readable sizes.
