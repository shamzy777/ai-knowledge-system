# HUMAN-REVIEW-002 — teaching presentation redesign

Date: 2026-11-24  
Status: applied

Human review requested a redesign of the validated Week 10 teaching presentation into a playful, visual, explorable continuous story while preserving the published-Wiki-only factual boundary, eleven section scopes, six quiz objectives, keyboard navigation, responsive behavior, and no-JavaScript fallback.

Applied decisions:

- Reuse one persistent sentence rather than introduce unrelated examples.
- Use a problem → mechanism → bridge rhythm in every section.
- Keep token embedding and positional information visually and conceptually separate.
- Preserve `Language Modeling → Large Language Model → Inference`.
- Teach Sampling as selection from a distribution; do not present it as the cause of Hallucination.
- Preserve a persistent nine-stage pipeline and visible Wiki mappings.
- Record redesign validation, failures, retries, and graph returns in new artifacts without rewriting prior logs.
