One canonical training concept

TAICA’s prediction-to-update mechanism and AI Programming’s Keras workflow are complementary theory ↔ practice views.

Workflow and controls

compile() configures loss, optimizer, and metrics. fit() runs batches and epochs; validation observes; evaluate() tests the trained model.

Conceptual parameter learning

Prediction → loss → backpropagation and gradient calculation → parameter update. Week 4 applies this framing to embeddings, Q/K/V, and other language-model parameters.
